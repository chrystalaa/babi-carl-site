Dear Carl Andrie...

| Prologue

My babi, my love, my weird future hubby, my handsome boyfriend, my all,
the one and only person who showed me that it is not impossible to love again.

At first, I really didn't know, babi, but I trusted my guts
I felt that you were someone special, and a part of me just instantly clicked with you.

Ikaw ang una nga nakita ko that I will be with until the end
At first, I thought it was only me feeling this way, and I thought I would just be another girl for you
Hadlok pa ko sang una nga basi we are not on the same page, pero I still risked it all.

Wala gid ko nagexpect nga you would like me back kag maseryoso ka gid man sa akon
And there it is, it was locked — I became all yours na, my love.

Look at us now babi. Time flies so fast and we've been together for so long na
We used to get excited just to go to school and see each other
Now we are on different campuses, but we still managed to go through it all.

And now, you're entering college na man just like me
Different or same campus, I'm always yours and I'll always be here for you, babi.

I am so proud of how far we've come, babi
But more than that, I am even more proud of how far you have come.

| A Letter

Sometimes I envy how wonderful you are
You know, there are things I see in you nga wala sa akon
Kag ikaw lang gid ang naghatag sakon sang amo ni nga pagpalangga.

But sometimes, I'm afraid that this wonderful person might be gone someday
Maybe that's why I'm always anxious to have your attention almost all the time
That I'm afraid that I can't be as wonderful and enough as you are.

I’m sorry for the misunderstandings and the lapses I had
I never meant to cause you confusion or hurt.

I always think about you, my love
And no matter what happens, I’m choosing to grow with you and understand you better
And I hope you'll truly be my first and last.

| Epilogue

Our story will never really end…
You didn't think this was it, did you?

Thank you for being you,
for being there,
and for being mine.

To more of us, my love.

I hope you always know nga ikaw lang gid akon, babi.
I love you, my Carl Andrie 💙

— Always yours, Tala 🤍
